        A second, separate AI reviews every trade the manager AI proposes and can
        veto any that isn't clearly justified by its research. This is the
        anti-“black box” check — you see the watchdog's verdict, not just an order.
      

      

      

    


    
    

      
📌 Position theses

      

        Why the AI owns each position, and the plan it set at entry. Every cycle it
        judges each holding against its own target and stop — so it takes
        profits and cuts losers instead of drifting.

🔬 Fundamental value scan

        
      

      

        A separate AI scan of each name's price vs. its latest-quarter fundamentals.

        rich = price ahead of fundamentals (avoid/trim). Refreshed about daily.
